Collected Memories is a digital archive of photographs that were taken on my moms old film camera. Documenting my family's life from California in the 1980s to Virginia. Preserving forgotten memories that sat untouched on a hard drive. Going through this collection, I noticed some common themes but also a pattern in how my family has dealt with grief. Hiding it and moving on, leaving their absences unspoken. This is a way for me to stop that generational pattern and honor the people and memories that still live within these photographs. Healing the trauma of treating grief as something that has to stay hidden.
